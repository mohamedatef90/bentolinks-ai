# Qlip brand guidelines

## Brand idea

Qlip is a calm, intelligent knowledge vault. It turns the links people collect
into material they can understand, revisit and use. The product should feel like
a focused reading room with an AI librarian—not another noisy bookmarking feed.

**Promise:** Save anything. Understand it later.

**Product line:** Your knowledge, ready when you are.

## Personality and voice

- **Clear:** short labels, concrete processing states, no AI theatre.
- **Calm:** useful confidence without urgency, hype or excessive animation.
- **Curious:** makes rediscovery and reading feel inviting.
- **Trustworthy:** separates extracted facts from inference and exposes failures.

Use sentence case. Prefer “AI summary” and “Reading link” to abstract technical
terms. Errors explain what happened and offer one next action.

## Visual identity

The Qlip mark is a clipped bookmark inside a soft square. It represents capture
and retrieval. The mark may appear alone at 24pt or larger; pair it with the Qlip
wordmark for primary navigation and onboarding.

### Color palette

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight | `#08131F` | Primary canvas |
| Deep panel | `#102131` | Cards and navigation surfaces |
| Raised panel | `#183044` | Selected and elevated surfaces |
| Qlip lime | `#B9E64B` | Primary action and brand signal |
| Knowledge mint | `#61D58A` | Success and completed processing |
| Warm amber | `#F2B84B` | Processing and attention |
| Cloud | `#F4F7F2` | Primary text on dark surfaces |
| Mist | `#9AABB7` | Secondary text |
| Hairline | `#294155` | Borders and separators |

Qlip lime is an accent, not a background flood. Dark neutrals occupy roughly
80% of the interface, raised surfaces 15%, and lime/mint/amber 5%.

## Typography

Use Apple system typography for native legibility and Dynamic Type support.
Rounded system display faces are reserved for the Qlip wordmark and major empty
states; editorial reading content uses the default system serif design where it
improves long-form comfort.

- Display: 34pt bold rounded, tight leading.
- Section title: 22pt semibold rounded.
- Card title: 17pt semibold.
- Body: 16–17pt regular, 1.5–1.65 line spacing.
- Metadata: 12–13pt medium, never below 12pt.

## Interface principles

- One dominant action per surface.
- Cards use 20–24pt radii, subtle hairlines and low shadows.
- Processing status always combines color, icon and text.
- Images support recognition but never replace title or source metadata.
- Minimum interactive target is 44pt.
- Motion is short and functional; respect Reduce Motion.
- Text contrast meets WCAG AA.

## Native component language

- **Vault card:** thumbnail/source mark, title, two-line summary, metadata, state.
- **Status chip:** small capsule with icon and semantic color.
- **Primary button:** lime fill, midnight text, 52pt height.
- **Secondary button:** deep-panel fill with hairline border.
- **AI summary:** raised panel with a lime spark marker, never a neon glow.
